# Rebuild Orraveth geography

## Why

Orraveth's compact map grew incrementally under an eight-move travel-day convention. A complete redesign can give its existing people, creatures and activities a coherent landscape, natural rivers, and journeys sized to the current 48-move authoring guideline.

## What Changes

- Rebuild the playable region's surface, settlement and underground layout from an approved terrain and drainage plan, using the supplied world-bible maps as design references.
- Preserve authored NPCs, creatures, dialogue, quests, objects, rewards, services and progression requirements. Retain meaningful place identities while replacing their coordinates and connections.
- Add river-design guidance covering valley descent, meanders, erosion and deposition, floodplains, crossings and settlement siting.
- Register the rebuilt pack as a fresh selectable Orraveth world instance. Keep the existing instance available for current characters.
- Add independently reviewed content-preservation coverage, route measurements and command-level journeys through the rebuilt map.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `orraveth-geography` covers terrain-driven layout, overland itineraries, access and retained content in the rebuilt world.
- `orraveth-world-editions` covers selecting and entering the fresh geography edition while retaining access to the existing world.

### Modified Capabilities

None. No related main specs currently exist.

## Impact

Affects the Orraveth pack, its layout/content assembly, world registration and selection, tests, and authoring documents. The scope is the existing playable region and its retained content. The fresh instance starts new character progress. Existing characters and historical events remain attached to the existing edition.
