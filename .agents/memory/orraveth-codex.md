---
name: Orraveth Codex separation
description: Project constraint keeping Orraveth lore separate from MUDD's existing demo world.
---

Orraveth is lore for a new, separate world. Keep its canon and gameplay state separate from the existing MUDD Waystone demo. Characters are bound to one world instance; an account can have a separate character and session in each world. See ADR 0022 for the ownership model.

Gold is Orraveth's provisional currency name. Denominations, prices, and exchange rates are not established.

Bellwold is intended to be a larger frontier settlement with more buildings, locations, and talkable residents. Specific new details remain provisional until approved.

Bellwold includes a wandering villager and a guard on a fixed public-street patrol. Their location is shared world state, not a client timer or per-character copy.

The south road outside Bellwold currently ends at unfinished roadwork. A small settlement at its end is a future plan, not a present location; do not add it until requested.

Town NPC dialogue should gradually reveal Bellwold's history and people, as well as wider Orraveth lore. Keep each account grounded in the speaker's experience, and attribute rumors or secondhand reports.

**Why:** the user clarified that Orraveth is a new world, named gold as its current currency, asked to expand Bellwold and its NPC conversations, and requested autonomous village movement; further economic and settlement details remain open.

**How to apply:** Keep Orraveth articles and its world pack standalone. Use gold only in Orraveth; do not invent prices, denominations, or exchange rules. Keep Bellwold additions connected, NPC knowledge local, and autonomous movement in shared world state. Mark new lore provisional and do not turn unresolved mysteries into established explanations.

**Why:** the user asked for an unfinished road now and a small settlement at its end later.

**How to apply:** Keep the current road end temporary; only add the settlement when the user asks to continue that route.
