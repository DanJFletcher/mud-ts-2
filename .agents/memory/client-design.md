---
name: Client design priorities
description: User direction for MUDD's gameplay UI, navigation, and mobile priority.
---

- Mobile is the most important layout.
- The game should have a tight, focused UI around what the player is doing and the actions they can take, not feel like a marketing website.
- Traveler info and Field notes should be separate screens the player navigates to.
- Marketing language can be reserved for the main home or login page, not gameplay.
- The Play room readout, event history, and command field form one terminal panel: history scrolls independently, the command stays docked, and Available paths remain separate.

**Why:** The user rejected the marketing-like gameplay presentation and unnecessary copy such as “No rush. Just the quiet work of being somewhere,” then approved the gameplay-first mobile direction with supporting information off the Play screen. The user also asked for a terminal-style, independently scrollable history with the command field at its bottom.

**How to apply:** Prioritize the mobile play experience, keep actionable game information prominent, use one terminal-style Play panel with a scrollable history and docked command field, and keep Available paths as separate quick actions.
