---
name: Room narration knowledge boundary
description: User's sensory-only standard for room descriptions and discovery of background information.
---

Room descriptions shown by look should be about what the player can see, smell, taste, hear, or feel, including one or two senses rather than all five. Do not include details the player could not otherwise know, such as which day a market is busy or what a room is used for. Information about a room should be gathered by reading or talking to NPCs.

**Why:** The user requested this standard for the mudd-game-master skill to improve room descriptions.

**How to apply:** Apply this boundary when authoring or reviewing player-facing room prose. Keep sensory observations separate from background knowledge disclosed through reading or NPC conversations.
