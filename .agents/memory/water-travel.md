---
name: Water travel
description: The intended access rule for river locations in MUDD.
---

River nodes must connect to one another through directional exits. They are accessible from land by boat when a land location has an exit connecting to the river.

**Why:** the user stated this as the intended river-travel rule.

**How to apply:** link adjacent river locations with exits and show those links on the map even before discovery. When land-to-river travel is implemented, require boat access and an authored exit from land to river.
