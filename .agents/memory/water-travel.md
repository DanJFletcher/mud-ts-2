---
name: Water travel
description: The intended access rule for river locations in MUDD.
---

River nodes are accessible by boat when a land location has an exit connecting to the river.

**Why:** the user stated this as the intended river-travel rule.

**How to apply:** when river travel is implemented, require both boat access and an authored land-to-river exit. Display-only river nodes must not become traversable merely because they appear on the map.
