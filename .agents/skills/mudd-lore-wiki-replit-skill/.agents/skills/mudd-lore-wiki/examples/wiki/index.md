# Example World Wiki

**Noncanonical training example.** The following fictional articles demonstrate how short, interconnected encyclopedia entries can preserve people, places, institutions and history without writing quests or game mechanics. They do not establish MUDD setting canon.

## Places and people

- [Lantern Reach](places/lantern-reach.md) — A harbor town defined by stone lights and uncertain maritime memory.
- [Isa Rehn](people/isa-rehn.md) — A keeper of civic records whose essays altered the town's historical reputation.

## Organizations and events

- [Wardens of the Lamp](factions/wardens-of-the-lamp.md) — The custodians of the shoreline lantern network.
- [The Night of Closed Lights](history/night-of-closed-lights.md) — A disputed night that reshaped coastal customs.
