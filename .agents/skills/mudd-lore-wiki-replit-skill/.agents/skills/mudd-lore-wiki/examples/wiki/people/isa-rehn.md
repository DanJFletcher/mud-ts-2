# Isa Rehn

**Isa Rehn** was a clerk and local historian of [Lantern Reach](../places/lantern-reach.md), best remembered for questioning the certainty of the town's most cherished maritime chronicles. Her work did not deny that the [Night of Closed Lights](../history/night-of-closed-lights.md) occurred; instead, she showed how few surviving records described the same sequence of events.

## Background and scholarship

Rehn learned to read harbor tallies in the Warden archives and eventually copied damaged civic registers into a new catalogue. Her marginal notes, unusually careful about naming sources, drew criticism from families who regarded the old accounts as sacred to the memory of lost sailors.

## Legacy

Later historians used Rehn's catalogue as a guide to conflicting testimony. The [Wardens of the Lamp](../factions/wardens-of-the-lamp.md), once skeptical of her conclusions, preserve her annotations alongside the original registers.
