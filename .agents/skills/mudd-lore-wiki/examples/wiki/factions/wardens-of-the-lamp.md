# Wardens of the Lamp

The **Wardens of the Lamp** are a civic fraternity responsible for the beacon houses of [Lantern Reach](../places/lantern-reach.md). Their authority began as an agreement among harbor families to maintain the town's lamps through the storm season and gradually expanded into custody of navigational and municipal records.

## Organization and traditions

Wardens are chosen from several harbor districts. Their robes bear a stitched circle of pale thread, a reference to the limestone rings surrounding each lamp. The fraternity keeps a yearly vigil in memory of [the Night of Closed Lights](../history/night-of-closed-lights.md).

## Records and interpretation

The fraternity preserves harbor ledgers and early testimonies, including the annotations of [Isa Rehn](../people/isa-rehn.md). Some members treat her comparisons as overdue scholarship, while others worry that questioning familiar accounts weakens communal remembrance.
