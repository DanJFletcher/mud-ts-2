# Lantern Reach

**Lantern Reach** is a harbor settlement at the mouth of the Greywash River, known for the pale limestone beacons lining its tidal causeway. Most buildings turn their narrow ends toward the prevailing sea winds, while covered courtyards shelter communal bread ovens and winter gardens.

## Origin and growth

The first permanent houses clustered beside a sheltered inlet where riverboats could unload without crossing the open shallows. The limestone lamps came later, after a series of winters in which fog made the shore difficult to distinguish from the water. Their construction gave the town its modern name and made the [Wardens of the Lamp](../factions/wardens-of-the-lamp.md) a powerful civic institution.

## Civic memory

The annual dusk procession marks [the Night of Closed Lights](../history/night-of-closed-lights.md), though residents disagree over whether the observance is a memorial or a warning. Historian [Isa Rehn](../people/isa-rehn.md) argued that the earliest surviving descriptions of the night were written decades after the event and deserve careful scrutiny.
