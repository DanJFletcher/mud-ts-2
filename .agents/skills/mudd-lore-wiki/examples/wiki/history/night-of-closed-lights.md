# The Night of Closed Lights

The **Night of Closed Lights** is the name given in [Lantern Reach](../places/lantern-reach.md) to an evening when the harbor beacons were said to have vanished behind a bank of silver fog. Local remembrance places the event early in the Lamp Years, although surviving records disagree about its date and duration.

## Recorded accounts

The oldest surviving harbor tally notes disrupted arrivals and an unusual closure of the landing slips. Later oral histories speak of lamps that continued burning but could not be seen from the water. Neither account by itself establishes why the beacons were invisible.

## Later remembrance

The annual dusk procession was formalized by the [Wardens of the Lamp](../factions/wardens-of-the-lamp.md). [Isa Rehn](../people/isa-rehn.md) compared the surviving testimonies and cautioned against treating the most dramatic retellings as eyewitness reports. Her critique remains part of the town's historical debate.
