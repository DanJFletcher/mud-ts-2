# Phase 3 Codex follow-ups

**Status: draft — awaiting Codex review.** These proposals describe the playable
layout on `content/bellwold-phase-3`; they are not additions to accepted canon.
No article under the Codex is edited by this phase. The Phase 2 follow-up is
outside this document's scope.

## Mosswake causeway and recognised territory edge

**Sources:** [Mosswake](../../../apps/web/src/codex/content/regions/mosswake.md),
[Tavin Reed](../../../apps/web/src/codex/content/people/tavin-reed.md), and the
[expansion spec](bellwold-region-expansion.md), §§3.3, 4.1–4.3.

**Suggested article targets:** Mosswake; Bellwold's regional travel account.

Proposed wording for review:

> A causeway kept by its users runs from the Far Bank Track near Bellwold to
> Mosswake's landing, through reed banks, short plank runs and firmer patches of
> ground. Families recognise some waters beyond the settlement's last planks as
> sprite territories, but there is no complete boundary map or shared account of
> access rights. The playable path ends at that edge; it does not enter the
> recognised territory.

The five causeway steps and exact platform arrangement are gameplay proposals.
The new eastern water tiles connect the Lower Rill to the landing's channel;
review that local adjacency without settling flow direction, wider hydrology,
or a connection to the Rillward Freeholds. The public dredging-survey notice is
an authored proposal, not a permit or a new commercial agreement. Sparse fishing
records no cause or settled extent for the reported decline.

## Thornmere footpath and shared bridge

**Status: draft — awaiting Codex review.** Sources:
[Thornmere](../../../apps/web/src/codex/content/regions/thornmere.md) and
[Nessa Valeen](../../../apps/web/src/codex/content/people/nessa-valeen.md).
Suggested article targets: Thornmere; the regional travel account.

> A footpath kept by the people who use it leaves the clearing near Bellwold's
> abandoned church, passes birches and a brook, and reaches Thornmere's lakeshore.
> Neighbours give differing accounts of sharing a small bridge with a sprite.
> Those accounts offer no universal agreement or promise that a visitor will
> receive an answer.

The five path steps, exact lake adjacency and placement of the council house
are gameplay proposals. Keep Thornmere independent and avoid implying that
Heskar constructed the path. Do not identify the offstage healer, announce a
medical-exception ruling, invent religious commitments for Nessa, or turn an
account of shared space into a new sprite-consent mechanic.
