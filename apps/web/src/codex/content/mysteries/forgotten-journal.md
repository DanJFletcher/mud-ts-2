Elira Venn's journal was discovered at the [[kindly-roof-inn|Kindly Roof Inn]]. Its final entries describe unusual measurements, gaps in the historical record, and a mysterious voice.

The archive's concealed account attributes the journal's placement to [[kindly-roof-hearth|Hearth]], the inn's resident sprite, who left it where someone could find it. This does not establish why Hearth chose to reveal it or what the voice was.

The journal may illuminate the [[silent-bell|Silent Bell]] investigation, but it is not a complete record of Elira's research. Its provenance, missing pages, and relationship to her disappearance remain open questions.
