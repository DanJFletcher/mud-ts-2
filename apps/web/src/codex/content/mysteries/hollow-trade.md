Evidence has emerged that some healing potions distributed through [[vells-crossing|Vell's Crossing]] contain [[veyl|Veyl]] obtained through destructive sprite essence extraction. Other preparations come from legitimate alchemical operations. The available record does not identify every affected batch or establish that low price alone indicates harmful provenance.

Commercial agents associated with [[severin-vale|Severin Vale]] operate in the town, but most residents do not know the full extent of those connections. The name attached to a company or shipment does not, by itself, establish who knew how a particular ingredient was obtained.

## Different risks, different responsibilities

Merchants and neighborhood delegates worry that an immediate prohibition could disrupt supply and livelihoods. Healers fear shortages for patients who cannot wait for alternatives. Religious leaders and advocates for sprite personhood call for transparent provenance and stronger protections. Researchers associated with the [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]] argue that non-extractive treatments deserve support, while acknowledging that no approach is yet a complete replacement.

The debate reaches beyond Vell's Crossing. [[mosswake|Mosswake]] residents are weighing a proposal to dredge wetlands where concentrated Veyl deposits have been found; [[thornmere|Thornmere]] prohibits commercial harvesting but is confronting a medical exception. These situations are related by the questions they raise, not proven to share a single supplier or cause.

The origin of particular potions, the extent of extraction, and the appropriate response remain contested. This account does not resolve the allegations or establish that all participants share the same knowledge or motives.
