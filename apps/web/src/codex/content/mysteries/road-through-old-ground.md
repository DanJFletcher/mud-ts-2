The Crown of Heskar has proposed a major road connecting [[greywatch|Greywatch]], [[bellwold|Bellwold]], and [[vells-crossing|Vell's Crossing]]. The project promises faster trade and military movement. Its planned route crosses land used or governed by independent communities and forest sprites, so the question is not only where a road can be built but who has the authority to approve it.

## Competing interests

Some merchants and residents expect a road to lower transport costs and make seasonal travel more dependable. Greywatch's officers see potential security benefits. Others fear that a route surveyed by Heskar will be treated as a claim over places where the Crown's authority is disputed.

Independent communities do not share one position. Some may seek negotiated access, compensation, or a rerouted section; others oppose construction through particular territories. People who live near the proposed corridor also have ordinary concerns about noise, work, property, and whether promised services will reach them.

[[oren-kest|Oren Kest]] objects to treating forest stands as available merely because they appear on a survey. [[nessa-valeen|Nessa Valeen]]'s experience with agreements between residents and sprites is relevant to questions of consent, but she does not speak for every community. Captain [[edric-vaul|Edric Vaul]] must reconcile the Crown's orders with conditions on the ground.

The route, construction schedule, and any negotiated outcome remain unsettled. No final boundary or universal agreement is established in this account.
