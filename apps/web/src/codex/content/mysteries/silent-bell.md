Bellwold's old bell historically rang before storms. Records also describe a period when it rang continuously and seventeen villagers disappeared. The bell is said to have come from the [[eastern-forest|eastern forest]].

When [[elira-venn|Professor Elira Venn]] began measuring the bell's [[resonance|Resonance]], it stopped ringing. The sequence is clear in the surviving account; causation is not. Elira later disappeared near Bellwold, and the bell's origin and nature remain unknown.

No single explanation accounts for the storm warnings, the disappearances, the change during measurement, and the bell's silence. The [[forgotten-journal|journal attributed to Elira]] may contain relevant observations, but its final entries raise further questions rather than resolving the mystery.
