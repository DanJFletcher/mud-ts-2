Anwen Marr owns an unusual balance scale at [[marrs-general-goods|Marr's General Goods]]. It sometimes produces measurements that cannot be reconciled with the known weights and materials.

A weak sprite may be [[sprite-anchoring|anchored]] to the scale, but the possibility is unconfirmed. Its exact abilities, if any, are unknown. The inconsistent readings could involve Resonance, a flaw in the instrument, or a factor not yet recorded.

The scale's mystery is a reminder that an impossible result is evidence of a problem to investigate, not immediate proof of a supernatural cause.
