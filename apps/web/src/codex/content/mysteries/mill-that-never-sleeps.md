Bellwold's abandoned watermill continues operating despite being deserted. A concealed apparatus there uses the mill's resident sprite for destructive essence extraction. An agent connected to [[severin-vale|Severin Vale]] operates the system, and the resulting [[veyl|Veyl]] enters the regional [[healing-potions|healing-potion supply chain]].

This is an established secret in the author's record, not common knowledge among Bellwold's residents. The agent's identity, the sprite's condition, and how long the operation has been running remain unspecified.

The mill links local infrastructure to a wider industry. Its operation raises questions about [[essence-harvesting|harvesting ethics]], the possible harm described as [[hollowing|Hollowing]], and the reliability of Veyl provenance.
