The Age of Measures is the name given to Orraveth's current intellectual period. Natural philosophers increasingly use repeatable observation and instruments to study Resonance, sprites, astronomy, and the ordinary physical world.

The name does not imply that earlier people lacked careful knowledge. Farmers, navigators, healers, craftspeople, and religious scholars have long gathered evidence through practice. What is changing is the scale and institutional authority of measurement, including who controls instruments, records, and the practical uses of discoveries.

The [[glass-collegium|Glass Collegium]] is one prominent center of this work, though its researchers disagree and depend on patrons. The [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]] tests whether medicine can reduce reliance on sprite essence. These projects create opportunity as well as political, economic, and theological tension.

No single calendar or boundary date for the Age of Measures is established here. Its beginning is a useful historical convention, not a universally accepted turning point.
