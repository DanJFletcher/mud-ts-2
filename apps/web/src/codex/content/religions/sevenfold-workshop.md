The Sevenfold Workshop tells of seven divine artisans who shaped an unfinished universe, each contributing different aspects of existence. The account makes craft a theological language: skilled work can be understood as participation in creation, though no single guild or temple owns that interpretation.

The True Pattern branch emphasizes an intended order that should be respected. The Living Forge branch teaches that mortals should continue and improve the work. Their disagreement appears in debates about inheritance, experimentation, and whether changing a long-standing practice is repair or presumption.

Followers may honor craft through shared meals, apprenticeships, careful maintenance, and public demonstrations of work. These practices differ by region and trade. The tradition offers an account of beginnings, not proof that the universe was literally constructed by seven beings.
