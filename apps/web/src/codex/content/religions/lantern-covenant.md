The Lantern Covenant teaches that Auren lit the First Lantern from its own essence, bringing light and existence into being. The image of a flame is used in household observances, public vigils, and arguments about responsibility; its meaning is not uniform across the tradition.

The Keepers of the Single Flame hold that Auren remains conscious and governs creation. Open Lanterns instead teach that Auren expended itself in creation, leaving the care of the world to mortals. Both branches inherit the same story but disagree about divine presence, obligation, and whether human authority can be claimed in Auren's name.

Covenant communities include careful scholars and reformers as well as custodians of established practice. Their views on [[sprites|sprites]] and [[resonance|Resonance]] vary: some regard them as signs of a sustaining order, while others caution that observable power says little about ultimate purpose. Neither interpretation is presented here as settled truth.
