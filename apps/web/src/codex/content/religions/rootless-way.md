The Rootless Way holds that existence emerged through natural processes that need not have been directed by a creator. Its practitioners may accept that gods and [[sprites|sprites]] exist while treating them as beings that could themselves have emerged within the world.

The Green Branch reveres nature and conscious landscapes and argues for protection grounded in relationship and responsibility. The Clear Branch prefers natural philosophy and skeptical inquiry to worship. These positions overlap in some communities and conflict in others; neither branch is a single political party.

Rootless teachers often distinguish respect from obedience and evidence from certainty. Their account of origins remains a worldview rather than a final proof. The continuing debate with other traditions is part of Orraveth's public intellectual life, especially in the [[age-of-measures|Age of Measures]].
