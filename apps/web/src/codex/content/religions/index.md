Orraveth's major traditions share questions but not answers. The [[lantern-covenant|Lantern Covenant]] describes a first light kindled by Auren; the [[sevenfold-workshop|Sevenfold Workshop]] remembers seven artisans shaping the universe; the [[returning-tide|Returning Tide]] teaches recurring creation and dissolution; the [[rootless-way|Rootless Way]] does not require an intentional creator.

Each tradition contains branches that disagree about how divine agency, change, memory, and human responsibility should be understood. These internal debates matter as much as the differences between faiths.

The traditions are recorded as beliefs, not as competing proofs of the universe's origin. Their teachings shape community practice and political life, but no one faith represents every region or person in Orraveth.
