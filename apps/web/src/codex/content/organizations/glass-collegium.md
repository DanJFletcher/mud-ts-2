The Glass Collegium is a scholarly institution associated with natural philosophy and the study of [[resonance|Resonance]]. Its name is linked to the careful use of glass instruments, including lenses and vessels used in observation.

The Collegium supports researchers, preserves records, and teaches methods for comparing results. It is not a single-minded authority: patrons, departments, and individual scholars disagree about what deserves funding and how openly findings should be shared.

During the [[age-of-measures|Age of Measures]], its influence has grown alongside demand for reliable instruments and technical expertise. [[elira-venn|Professor Elira Venn]] worked as a natural philosopher, while [[severin-vale|Severin Vale]] is associated with more ethically contentious research. Institutional affiliation does not imply agreement with every project.

The Bellwold Marches have drawn scholarly attention because of questions about [[resonance|Resonance]], sprites, and the provenance of alchemical goods. The Collegium is not a single authority on the [[hollow-trade|Hollow Trade]] or the [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]]; researchers and patrons disagree about evidence, consent, and the pace at which alternative treatments should be made available.
