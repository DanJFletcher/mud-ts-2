Orraveth has no single legal definition of a sprite that applies across every polity. Local rules may treat a sprite as a person, a protected presence, a sacred being, a dangerous force, or a resource. These categories can overlap and often reflect the interests of the institutions that enforce them.

Religious traditions do not speak with one voice. Some followers see sprites as signs of a larger order; others treat them as beings within nature, worthy of care without worship. Natural philosophers may seek repeatable evidence while disagreeing about what evidence could establish personhood.

The practical point is that a permit, contract, or classification made in one jurisdiction may not travel with a sprite across a border. Accounts of [[sprite-consent-and-personhood|consent]] should be kept distinct from local law, and law distinct from what a community believes.
