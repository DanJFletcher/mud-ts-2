The Widow of the River is a name attached to accounts of a sprite associated with a river. Stories describe warnings, reflections that do not match their surroundings, and an old grief attributed to the being. These details vary from telling to telling.

The name should not be read as evidence of a human marriage or a known biography. Some communities treat it as a respectful title; others regard the figure as a metaphor for dangerous water or a warning against taking too much from a river.

No single account has been verified as a complete history. The entry is retained because it documents living belief and the ways communities interpret encounters with [[sprites|sprites]].
