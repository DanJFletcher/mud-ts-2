Old Silverfin is the name used by some boat crews for a river sprite associated with the crossing at [[rillford|Rillford]]. The archive has no independent description of its form or account of how it acquired the name.

Riverside accounts say Old Silverfin has begun overturning boats carrying certain cargoes. The descriptions differ, and no verified list identifies what those cargoes share. Some crews interpret the incidents as a warning; others suspect poor loading, hazardous goods, or deliberate interference by people who wish to influence the ferry dispute.

Guildmistress [[selda-rook|Selda Rook]] has called for careful records before assigning a cause. That caution does not establish whether the sprite is acting intentionally or what it might want. No known agreement, conversation, or reliable statement from Old Silverfin is recorded.

The name should be treated as local usage rather than a formal classification. The account remains unresolved, and the sprite's nature, motives, relationship to the [[lower-rill|Lower Rill]], and connection to any commercial dispute are unknown.
