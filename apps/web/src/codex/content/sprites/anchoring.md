Anchoring is the name for a connection that associates a sprite's presence or activity with a place, object, or other stable reference. It is studied alongside [[binding|Binding]], since lasting magical effects require stable anchors under the [[resonance-laws|practical laws of Resonance]].

Anchoring may be voluntary, inherited, imposed, or simply observed without a known cause. A connection's persistence does not establish consent. Researchers should distinguish a sprite choosing to remain from a pattern that restricts its movement, and should record what evidence supports either interpretation.

Anchors can be damaged, moved, or altered, with consequences that are not always predictable. The ethical and legal status of such acts depends in part on whether the sprite can communicate and what it has expressed. This makes anchoring a technical subject and a question of [[sprite-consent-and-personhood|personhood]].
