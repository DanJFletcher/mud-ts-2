Researchers commonly describe observed manifestation in five stages:

- **Dormant:** suspected presence, with no clear response.
- **Whispering:** faint effects or signals, often difficult to distinguish from chance.
- **Manifesting:** repeatable influence on the material world.
- **Embodied:** a stable form that can act in a physical environment.
- **Sovereign:** a working label for a highly capable or apparently independent being.

These terms describe observed capability, not a proven ladder of evolution. Classification can change with evidence, and it does not answer whether a being has interests or deserves protection. A quiet sprite is not necessarily harmless; a powerful one is not necessarily a god.

The system is most useful when reports include the conditions of observation, the limits of the observer's knowledge, and whether a sprite could choose how to respond.
