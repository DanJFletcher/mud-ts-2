Some embodied sprites can communicate, express preferences, and refuse. Whether every form of sprite presence has comparable awareness remains uncertain; the [[sprite-classifications|classification vocabulary]] does not resolve that question.

The central ethical question is how much evidence of agency is enough to require consent. Many practitioners treat clear refusal as decisive. Others argue that uncertainty itself warrants restraint, particularly when a being is [[sprite-anchoring|anchored]] or cannot leave. Institutions that regard sprite essence as a resource have economic incentives to set a lower bar.

This debate is not settled by a sprite's power, origin, or usefulness. The accounts of the [[sword-called-mercy|Sword Called Mercy]] and other embodied sprites are often cited, but individual testimony should not be generalized to every being.
