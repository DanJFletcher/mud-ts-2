Sprites are nonhuman beings associated with [[resonance|Resonance]]. The word covers a wide range of phenomena and persons, from faint presences that may never be shown to think, to embodied intelligences capable of speech, negotiation, memory, and refusal. Their differences make a single legal or ethical rule difficult to apply.

Researchers use a working [[sprite-classifications|classification system]] based on how a sprite manifests. A sprite may be associated with a place or object through [[sprite-anchoring|anchoring]], but the nature of that connection is not always understood.

Questions of [[sprite-consent-and-personhood|consent and personhood]] and [[sprite-law-and-culture|legal status and cultural belief]] are active debates. This archive treats a sprite's apparent power as no proof of divine origin, and uncertainty about its nature as no proof that it lacks interests.

Four often-discussed examples are the [[sword-called-mercy|Sword Called Mercy]], [[house-that-waits|the House That Waits]], the [[widow-of-river|Widow of the River]], and the [[kindly-roof-hearth|Hearth of the Kindly Roof]]. Their accounts do not establish a single origin for spritekind.
