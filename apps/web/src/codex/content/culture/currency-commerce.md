Commerce in Orraveth involves market exchange, contracts, credit, and the movement of goods. Trading customs differ between the [[merefall-league|Merefall League's city-states]], frontier villages such as [[bellwold|Bellwold]], and communities whose agreements are maintained through councils.

Gold is the current provisional currency name used in Orraveth. This establishes neither a system of denominations nor a universal weight, value, exchange rate, or level of acceptance between regions.

[[bellwold|Bellwold]] is the first settlement using this convention. The prices and accounting practices at [[marrs-general-goods|Anwen Marr's shop]] and the village market remain unspecified. A familiar-looking coin does not guarantee identical weight, authority, or value elsewhere.
