Commerce in Orraveth involves market exchange, contracts, credit, and the movement of goods. Trading customs differ between the [[merefall-league|Merefall League's city-states]], frontier villages such as [[bellwold|Bellwold]], and communities whose agreements are maintained through councils.

No standard currency name, denomination, or exchange rate has been fixed in the current canon. Regional coinage may exist, but the Codex does not assign invented values or assume that every settlement accepts the same money.

Anwen Marr's shop and Bellwold's market are useful local settings for future detail, but their prices and accounting practices remain open. A familiar-looking coin does not guarantee identical weight, authority, or value in another region.
