Orraveth's knowledge is preserved through formal study, apprenticeship, correspondence, oral teaching, and records kept by families, temples, guilds, and institutions. Literacy and access to formal schooling vary; the existence of a university does not mean every person can attend.

The [[sareth-cantons|Sareth Cantons]] are associated with universities, and the [[glass-collegium|Glass Collegium]] supports study of natural philosophy. At the same time, practical knowledge held by healers, growers, craftspeople, and travelers may be absent from institutional archives.

Records can be incomplete, selective, or missing. The gaps in [[elira-venn|Elira Venn's research]] and the [[forgotten-journal|journal attributed to her]] show how absence itself can become part of a mystery.
