Most people experience Orraveth through the work that sustains a household and the relationships that make that work possible. Food must be grown, stored, traded, or gathered; tools need repair; care is shared among families, neighbors, healers, and institutions.

Life varies sharply by region. A farmer near [[bellwold|Bellwold]] depends on seasonal work and a frontier market, while a Sareth craftsperson may be shaped by mining and university life. River communities in Rillward negotiate access through reciprocal agreements.

The Age of Measures brings new instruments and research, but it does not replace practical knowledge. A complete account of daily life must include differences in wealth, literacy, mobility, faith, and access to land rather than presenting one custom as universal.
