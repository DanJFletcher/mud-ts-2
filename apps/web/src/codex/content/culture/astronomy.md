Astronomers in Orraveth observe planets, stars, and other celestial phenomena with instruments and inherited records. Their measurements may support calendars, navigation, and debate about the physical universe.

The [[age-of-measures|Age of Measures]] has increased interest in repeatable observation. The [[glass-collegium|Glass Collegium]] investigates astronomy alongside Resonance and natural philosophy, but its instruments do not provide a complete account of the cosmos.

Observation can constrain a theory without proving a particular creation story. Competing religious accounts remain interpretations of the universe's origin, not conclusions derived from a single chart of the sky.
