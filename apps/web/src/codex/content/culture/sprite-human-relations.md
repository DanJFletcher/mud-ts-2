Relationships between people and sprites range from cooperative to coercive. Some communities share space with a sprite without claiming ownership; some practitioners negotiate for help; commercial systems may treat essence as a resource even when a sprite can refuse.

The ethical status of a relationship depends on agency, communication, power, and the ability to leave. A long-standing connection or mutual benefit does not automatically establish consent. The [[symbiotic-school|Symbiotic School]] explores cooperative practice, while [[essence-harvesting|harvesting systems]] reveal the pressures created by demand.

People's religious and cultural interpretations differ. This archive records those beliefs without asserting that any one tradition has explained what sprites are.
