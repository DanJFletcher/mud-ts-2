Farming is part of Bellwold's stated economy and a foundation of everyday life across many regions. Crops, storage, livestock, and access to water vary with climate and local knowledge; the Codex does not yet establish a universal list of staple foods.

Harvests connect villages to transport and trade. A poor season can affect prices, migration, and the availability of ingredients used by healers and the [[botanical-school|Botanical School]].

Food traditions should be developed region by region. The absence of a dish from a written record does not mean people do not cook it, and a crop suited to one part of Orraveth should not be assumed to grow everywhere.
