Orraveth contains communities with different languages, histories, livelihoods, and relationships to authority. A practice described as normal in one settlement may be unfamiliar or objectionable elsewhere.

Local custom can organize hospitality, land access, conflict resolution, work, and obligations to neighbors. The [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]] rely on negotiated councils and reciprocal rights; communities in the [[uncrowned-lands|Uncrowned Lands]] are not defined by the names outside powers give them.

These entries do not attempt to assign one personality to an entire nation or people. Cultural details should be attributed to the community that practices them and should leave room for disagreement within that community.
