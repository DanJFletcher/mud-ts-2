Land in Orraveth can be understood through legal title, inherited use, communal access, negotiated agreement, or spiritual relationship. These claims may conflict, especially where a state presses its frontier into territory used by independent communities.

The [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]] has strong property laws, while the [[uncrowned-lands|Uncrowned Lands]] include peoples and territories outside centralized national control. Neither fact means that all land is privately owned or that every unclaimed parcel is available for settlement.

The current archive does not define a universal property code. Any future account of a boundary should identify whose map, testimony, or law is being used.
