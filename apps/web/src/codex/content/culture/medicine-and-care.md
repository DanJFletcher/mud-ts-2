Medicine in Orraveth combines local healing knowledge, care from family and neighbors, craft, and alchemical preparations. Availability depends on distance, wealth, training, and the reliability of trade.

[[healing-potions|Healing potions]] can accelerate recovery, often through [[veyl|Veyl]], but they are not a universal cure. The [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]] studies expensive, experimental alternatives. Bellwold's [[green-mortar|Green Mortar]], associated with [[ilya-fen|Ilya Fen]], represents local care whose exact practices are not yet recorded.

Medical claims should identify the strength of evidence and the source of ingredients. The history of healing is not a choice between “old” and “new”; responsible care can draw on both practical experience and controlled study.
