“Frontier” describes a relationship to claims and authority, not an empty strip of land. A border can matter to a government, a traveler, a household, and a neighboring community in different ways. The archive does not provide a complete map of Orraveth, and the absence of a recorded boundary is not proof that no community lives there.

These differences are visible across the continent. The [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]] emphasizes property law, rigid borders, and expansionist interests. The [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]] negotiate river access through councils and reciprocal rights. In the [[uncrowned-lands|Uncrowned Lands]], independent peoples, settlements, and sprite territories retain their own histories and claims. The terms used for those lands—“Wild,” “Open Marches,” “Old Ground,” “Free Soil,” or “Breathing Lands”—often disclose the speaker's assumptions.

For a settlement near a frontier, practical work may depend on trade, travel, land use, and records, but those needs do not settle questions of ownership or jurisdiction. [[bellwold|Bellwold]] is near the Heskar frontier; its exact legal status remains unestablished.

This article is a provisional synthesis. It should not be used to infer a universal frontier law or a single pattern of life shared by every border community.
