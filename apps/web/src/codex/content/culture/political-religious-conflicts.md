Political and religious conflict in Orraveth is rarely only about doctrine. It can concern who controls land, who records history, which institutions fund research, and whether a sprite may be treated as a source of medicine.

The [[veyrun-concord|Veyrun Concord]] balances temple and civic authority; the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]] holds strong property law and expansionist frontier interests; the [[merefall-league|Merefall League]] gives commercial guilds influence. These structures create different pressures without making every member of a community agree.

Debates over [[essence-harvesting|essence harvesting]] bring ethics and economics into direct conflict. The Codex records positions and evidence separately from rumor, and does not treat a political claim as a neutral description of fact.
