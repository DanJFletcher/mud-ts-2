Craft and land are sources of livelihood and political power. Apprenticeship, guild membership, workshop ownership, mining rights, and access to shared resources vary between regions and trades.

The [[merefall-league|Merefall League]] gives guilds strong commercial influence; the [[sareth-cantons|Sareth Cantons]] are known for mining and skilled craft; the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]] enforces strong property law. These arrangements shape who can practice a trade and who benefits from its results.

No single guild charter or ownership code is established across Orraveth. Bellwold's [[blackwater-forge|Blackwater Forge]] is one local workshop, not a representative of every smithing tradition.
