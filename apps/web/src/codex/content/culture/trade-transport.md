Orraveth's trade moves through roads, rivers, local markets, and the knowledge of travelers. Routes are shaped by weather, terrain, safety, tolls, and the agreements of communities along the way.

The [[merefall-league|Merefall League]] depends on commercial networks, while the [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]] negotiate river access through local councils and reciprocal rights. A traveling merchant such as [[jorek-vass|Jorek Vass]] links a village like [[bellwold|Bellwold]] to places beyond its immediate reach.

The form of travel differs by region. In Heskar, rigid borders and strong property law make land claims consequential; the [[merefall-league|Merefall League]] organizes much commercial life around guild-influenced city-states. In the [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]], river access is negotiated through councils and reciprocal rights. Those differences affect what a traveler must learn before setting out; they do not provide a single continent-wide rule.

No complete route map or universal schedule is established. Distance, tolls, and access should remain local facts, not be invented from a generic map of the continent. [[bellwold|Bellwold]] sits near Heskar's frontier, but neither the village's precise jurisdiction nor every route beyond it is established.

A provisional south road leaves Bellwold, crosses the [[lower-rill|Lower Rill]] by a stone bridge, and ends beyond the far bank where the roadwork stops. No settlement has been established at its end, and the route gives no basis for assuming a destination or travel time.

The west road runs from the Kindly Roof to a verge beyond the village fields, where the Harrowfen Road continues beside a drainage ditch to [[harrowfen|Harrowfen]]'s field gate, about half a day from Bellwold. The eastbound mill road is mapped provisionally: it passes through the abandoned watermill before ending beyond a low rise, and its onward destination and travel time are not established.
