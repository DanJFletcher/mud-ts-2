Orraveth's inhabitants observe a universe containing planets, stars, and other astronomical phenomena. Their explanations remain constrained by medieval instruments, inherited records, and the practical demands of navigation, agriculture, ritual, and scholarship.

Four traditions offer markedly different accounts. The [[lantern-covenant|Lantern Covenant]] remembers a first light kindled by Auren; the [[sevenfold-workshop|Sevenfold Workshop]] speaks of artisans shaping unfinished existence; the [[returning-tide|Returning Tide]] describes recurring creation and dissolution; the [[rootless-way|Rootless Way]] does not require an intentional creator. These are documented beliefs, not verified histories of the universe.

The physical existence of [[sprites|sprites]], the repeatability of [[resonance|Resonance]], and the presence of beings called gods by some traditions do not prove which account is correct. Astronomers can refine observations without answering questions beyond the reach of their instruments. The origin of the universe remains unresolved.
