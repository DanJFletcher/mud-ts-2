Orraveth is the main continent and setting of this Codex. Its inhabitants live in a universe of planets, stars, and other physical phenomena, though their instruments and inherited cosmologies provide only a partial account of that universe.

Resonance works consistently enough to be studied, and sprites can demonstrate agency and influence the material world. Neither observation establishes a single explanation for creation, consciousness, or the origin of magic. Religious traditions, natural philosophers, and local communities interpret the same evidence differently.

The present is often called the [[age-of-measures|Age of Measures]]. Its growth of measurement and experimental practice has brought new medicines and instruments, but also pressure on institutions, labor, and belief. Practical knowledge remains distributed among healers, farmers, craftspeople, navigators, and scholars; formal institutions do not contain every useful record.

Orraveth is not governed by one shared faith or political order. The [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]] is a hereditary monarchy with strict property law and rigid borders. The [[merefall-league|Merefall League]] is a network of merchant city-states where guilds hold considerable influence; the [[veyrun-concord|Veyrun Concord]] balances temple and civic authority. The [[sareth-cantons|Sareth Cantons]] are mountain communities with local assemblies, universities, mining, and skilled craft. In the [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]], river communities and mobile groups negotiate access through councils and reciprocal rights. These arrangements are not interchangeable or stages of a single political order; the [[nations-and-frontiers|regional directory]] offers a fuller comparison.

Beyond centralized state claims, the [[uncrowned-lands|Uncrowned Lands]] contain communities with their own histories and claims. The name itself changes with the speaker: “Wild,” “Open Marches,” “Old Ground,” “Free Soil,” and “Breathing Lands” are not neutral synonyms. A frontier is not an empty place simply because a map or government leaves it unresolved; see [[frontier-settlements|frontier settlements and contested edges]].

Travel connects these different societies through roads, rivers, local markets, and personal agreements. Routes, tolls, access rights, and even the meaning of a border depend on the communities involved. The current provisional currency name is gold; it does not establish a universal unit of value or a shared exchange system.

The village of [[bellwold|Bellwold]] is one frontier settlement among many, not the center of the continent. Its old bell and surrounding records remain subjects of disagreement rather than a solved account of Orraveth's past.

The [[currency-and-commerce|Codex entry on currency and commerce]] leaves gold's denominations and interregional exchange unsettled.
