Veiling studies effects on perception and illusion. Accounts of a veil may involve altered sensory experience, selective attention, or disagreements among observers about what was present. These are not interchangeable explanations, and careful reports distinguish what a witness perceived from what an instrument recorded.

The discipline's name has also entered ordinary speech as a metaphor for secrecy, leading to occasional confusion in court records and personal testimony. Veiling's practical limits are studied alongside range, duration, pattern, and resistance described in the [[resonance-laws|laws of Resonance]].

The existence of a perception effect does not prove a particular theory of consciousness or the metaphysical nature of [[sprites|sprites]].
