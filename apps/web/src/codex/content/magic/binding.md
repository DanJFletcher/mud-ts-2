Binding concerns connections, wards, and magical anchors. A lasting effect requires a stable point of reference, according to the practical [[resonance-laws|laws of Resonance]]. That observation makes Binding important in both laboratory practice and discussions about [[sprite-anchoring|sprites anchored to places or objects]].

The word can describe a technical connection, a protective working, or a coercive act. Writers should specify which meaning they intend. A stable anchor does not by itself imply consent, personhood, or harmlessness; those questions are addressed separately in discussions of [[sprite-consent-and-personhood|sprite consent and personhood]].

Binding's history is recorded unevenly, since some methods have been kept within families, workshops, or religious institutions.
