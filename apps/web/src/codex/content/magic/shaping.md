Shaping concerns the manipulation of physical matter through [[resonance|Resonance]]. Its practitioners work with the properties and limitations of existing material, and their records pay close attention to mass, resistance, and the complexity of a change.

The discipline has long-standing relationships with craftspeople, though a trained artisan and a Resonance scholar do not necessarily share the same methods or priorities. Workshops may treat practical results as sufficient, while the [[glass-collegium|Glass Collegium]] often asks for controlled comparison and written measures.

Shaping is not a license for unlimited creation. Its observed practice remains consistent with the [[resonance-laws|practical laws of Resonance]].
