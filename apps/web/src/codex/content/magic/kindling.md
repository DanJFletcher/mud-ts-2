Kindling is the practical discipline of [[resonance|Resonance]] concerned with energy and elemental effects. Its practitioners study how patterns direct existing energy and how materials, distance, and duration change the result.

The name is sometimes used more broadly in everyday speech for any dramatic magical display, which can obscure the discipline's careful limits. A visible flame or sudden heat does not demonstrate that matter appeared from nothing; recorded practice is interpreted through the [[resonance-laws|laws of Resonance]].

Training traditions differ over whether Kindling belongs first to craft, natural philosophy, or devotional practice. No single explanation of its ultimate source is established.
