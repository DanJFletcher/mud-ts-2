Researchers and practitioners commonly summarize observed Resonance through five practical constraints:

1. Magical effects require patterns.
2. Change requires energy or resources.
3. Range, mass, duration, complexity, and resistance constrain an effect.
4. Lasting enchantments require stable anchors.
5. Known magic transforms existing matter and energy rather than freely creating unlimited resources.

These statements are working principles, not a complete theory of the phenomenon. Measurements can be difficult to compare when instruments, materials, or local conditions differ. A failed replication may reveal a missing condition rather than disprove an entire discipline.

The laws describe observed limits. They do not settle the origin of [[resonance|Resonance]], whether every apparent exception has the same cause, or how these observations fit any religious account.
