Resonance is the common name for magic in Orraveth. Its effects are mechanically consistent enough for practitioners to compare observations, teach disciplined methods, and record failures. The name describes a practical field of study; it does not explain why the phenomenon exists.

Five widely recognized constraints are collected in [[resonance-laws|the practical laws of Resonance]]. They describe the need for patterns, the cost of change, limits on scale and resistance, the role of stable anchors, and the transformation of existing matter and energy rather than unlimited creation.

Five practical disciplines organize work: [[kindling|Kindling]], [[shaping|Shaping]], [[veiling|Veiling]], [[binding|Binding]], and [[listening|Listening]]. These are traditions of practice, not necessarily separate forces of nature. Their boundaries and teaching methods are debated among institutions such as the [[glass-collegium|Glass Collegium]].

Resonance's origin remains unknown. A successful experiment can establish what happened under recorded conditions; it cannot by itself establish which creation account is true.
