Bellwold is a small trading village near the frontier of the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]]. Its economy includes farming, milling, blacksmithing, trade, lodging, and small-scale crafting.

The current Bellwold record uses [[currency-and-commerce|gold]] as Orraveth's provisional named currency. Local prices, accounting practices, and any relationship to regional coinage remain unspecified.

The village's daily life gathers around the [[bellwold-market-square|market square]], the [[kindly-roof-inn|Kindly Roof Inn]], [[marrs-general-goods|Marr's General Goods]], [[blackwater-forge|Blackwater Forge]], and [[green-mortar|the Green Mortar]]. Nearby are the [[old-bell-tower|old bell tower]], [[eastern-forest|eastern forest]], and [[abandoned-watermill|abandoned watermill]].

Bellwold's records are incomplete. The old bell, the disappearance of [[elira-venn|Professor Elira Venn]], and the [[mill-that-never-sleeps|watermill's continued operation]] have made it a place of scholarly attention and local unease.
