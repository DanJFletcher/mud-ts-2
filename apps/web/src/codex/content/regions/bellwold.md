Bellwold is a growing trading settlement near the frontier of the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]]. Farming, milling, blacksmithing, trade, lodging, and small-scale craft support its residents and the travelers who pass through.

The current Bellwold record uses [[currency-and-commerce|gold]] as Orraveth's provisional named currency. Local prices, accounting practices, and any relationship to regional coinage remain unspecified.

The market square and its nearby [[kindly-roof-inn|inn]] and [[marrs-general-goods|general store]] remain the settlement's busiest point. South of them, [[bellwold-south-common|South Common]] opens onto [[well-court|Well Court]], [[wattle-row|Wattle Row]], and several shared and residential buildings. [[blackwater-forge|Blackwater Forge]] and [[green-mortar|the Green Mortar]] serve practical needs, while the [[bellwold-common-house|Common House]] keeps public notices and records. The exact arrangement of these newer streets and buildings remains provisional.

[[linnet-reed|Linnet Reed]] is often seen walking between the square, the well, and the public lanes. [[taren-voss|Taren Voss]] keeps a regular watch circuit from the tower road through the southern public spaces.

Bellwold has no single established founding date or continuous town chronicle in the current record. The provisional page on [[bellwold-records|Bellwold's fragmentary records]] compares the village's practical tallies with the older accounts surrounding the silent bell.

The current Bellwold record uses [[currency-and-commerce|gold]] as Orraveth's provisional named currency. Local prices, accounting practices, and any relationship to regional coinage remain unspecified.

Nearby are the [[old-bell-tower|old bell tower]], [[eastern-forest|eastern forest]], and [[abandoned-watermill|abandoned watermill]]. The surrounding roads and field edges are home to animals described in the provisional record of [[bellwold-roadside-wildlife|Bellwold roadside wildlife]].

An unfinished road leaves south from the Common House, crosses the [[lower-rill|Lower Rill]] by a low stone bridge, and continues for several rooms beyond the far bank. It currently ends at a shallow cut in the ground; no settlement is established beyond it. The road's eventual destination remains unrecorded.

The west road leaves from the Kindly Roof and reaches a verge beyond the village fields. There it continues as the Harrowfen Road, a rutted farm road beside a drainage ditch, to the field gate of [[harrowfen|Harrowfen]], about half a day's travel west. The eastbound mill road branches from Old Bell Tower Road, passes through the abandoned watermill, and ends beyond a low rise. It does not establish a destination beyond its mapped endpoint.

Bellwold's records are incomplete. The old bell, the disappearance of [[elira-venn|Professor Elira Venn]], and the [[mill-that-never-sleeps|watermill's continued operation]] have made it a place of scholarly attention and local unease.

## The surrounding region

Bellwold is an important local market within the provisional [[bellwold-marches|Bellwold Marches]], also called the Lower Rill by some. It is not the region's largest settlement: [[rillford|Rillford]] and [[vells-crossing|Vell's Crossing]] are larger trading centers. Bellwold sits between Heskar's settled territory and the [[uncrowned-lands|Uncrowned Lands]], but its exact legal status is not established.

The village's regional ties are practical rather than a single political alliance. [[harrowfen|Harrowfen]] supplies food and flax; [[ashcombe|Ashcombe]] produces timber and charcoal; Rillford connects river trade; and [[greywatch|Greywatch]] represents Heskar's military administration. [[thornmere|Thornmere]] shares woodland goods and sprite-related knowledge; Vell's Crossing distributes imported goods and alchemical products; [[mosswake|Mosswake]] trades fish, reeds, and medicinal plants; and [[elderhollow|Elderhollow]] is associated with specialized crafts and historical knowledge. These relationships and the regional geography remain provisional.
