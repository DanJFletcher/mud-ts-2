The Sareth Cantons are mountain communities known for local assemblies, universities, mining interests, and skilled craftsmanship. Their politics are organized closer to the community than the centralized monarchy of Heskar.

Mining and craft create both wealth and obligations: decisions about labor, water, and access to materials affect people beyond one workshop. The details of each canton's laws and relationship to the universities are not identical or fully documented.

The Sareth Cantons are one of several distinct political arrangements in Orraveth, not a model that can be applied to every mountain community.
