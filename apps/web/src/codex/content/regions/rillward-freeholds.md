The Rillward Freeholds include river settlements and mobile communities whose governance relies on negotiated councils and reciprocal rights. Authority is maintained through relationships and agreements rather than a single permanent hierarchy.

Access to a landing, shared water, or seasonal route may depend on obligations remembered by several communities. A traveler cannot assume that one council speaks for every group using a river.

Rillward practice makes transportation and local custom closely connected. Its agreements are not a universal code, and the exact boundaries of the Freeholds are not fixed in this directory.
