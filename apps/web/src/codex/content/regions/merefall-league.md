The Merefall League is a collection of merchant city-states dominated by guilds and commercial interests. Its strength lies in networks of trade, contract, and specialized labor rather than one uniform central administration.

League cities may share interests while competing over routes, tariffs, and influence. The balance between civic offices and guild authority is not described as identical in every member city.

The League's currency, exact membership, and borders remain open for future specification. Its commercial influence is part of the wider [[trade-and-transport|trade network]] of Orraveth.
