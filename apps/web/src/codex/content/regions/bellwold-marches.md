The Bellwold Marches is a provisional name for the frontier region surrounding [[bellwold|Bellwold]], near the eastern edge of the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]]. Some accounts call the broader area the Lower Rill, after the river corridor that passes near the village. Neither name establishes a fixed jurisdiction or boundary.

The region brings together Heskar communities, independent settlements, trading centers, farmland, woodland, and wetlands. Bellwold is an important local market and milling stop, but it is smaller than [[rillford|Rillford]] and [[vells-crossing|Vell's Crossing]]. Residents disagree about sovereignty, land use, trade, sprite personhood, and how far Heskar's authority should extend.

The river near Bellwold is provisionally called the Lower Rill, but its full course and relationship to the [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]] are not established.

## Trade and shared pressures

The region's economies are interdependent without being governed by one market. Harrowfen's farms supply food and flax; Ashcombe's woodworkers produce timber and charcoal; Rillford handles river commerce; and Vell's Crossing distributes imported and alchemical goods. Bellwold provides milling services, local crafts, and market exchange. Thornmere, Mosswake, and Elderhollow contribute woodland knowledge, marsh products, specialized crafts, and histories, though not every resident agrees that these should be treated as commodities.
