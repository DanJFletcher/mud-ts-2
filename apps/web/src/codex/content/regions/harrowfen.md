Harrowfen is an agricultural settlement of approximately 340 people, about half a day's travel west of [[bellwold|Bellwold]]. It lies in the settled territory of the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]], though much of its farmland belongs to Heskar noble families rather than the people who work it.

## Land, government, and work

The surrounding low fields and drainage cuts support grain, root vegetables, and flax. A shared threshing green and village weigh-house serve as practical meeting places; their exact form and history remain provisional. Harrowfen's elected reeve handles village business and negotiates with landholders and neighboring markets.

Reeve [[maela-thorn|Maela Thorn]] grew up in agricultural labor and now speaks for farmers in local disputes. Her position does not remove the difference between tenants, landowners, hired workers, and households with small plots. Residents disagree about how much authority an elected reeve can exercise when formal ownership rests elsewhere.

## Customs and belief

Harrowfen's year follows planting, drainage, harvest, and the work of repairing tools. Households keep practical records of seed, weather, and yields, while local observances vary between families and followings. Some farmers describe good soil as a gift to be tended; others emphasize inherited labor and careful management. No single religious practice defines the village.

The settlement's founding date is not recorded. Its present life is organized around the fields, shared threshing green, and the work of keeping drainage and tools serviceable.

## Current concerns

Recent harvests have declined despite weather many residents consider favorable. Some attribute the change to depleted soil or altered water management; others wonder whether local [[resonance|Resonance]] or nearby [[veyl|Veyl]] extraction has affected the land. These are competing explanations, not findings. The cause remains unresolved in [[vanishing-grain|the Vanishing Grain]] account.

Harrowfen sends food and flax toward Bellwold and trades through [[rillford|Rillford]]. It relies on regional smiths and repairers for tools, while its dependence on outside food and goods varies by household and season. The village's population, travel time, and regional role are provisional.
