The Veyrun Concord is a federation in which religious institutions hold substantial influence while civic authority remains part of the political balance. The word “Concord” points to negotiation, not an absence of disagreement.

Temple and civic leaders may cooperate on public care, education, and dispute resolution while contesting who has final authority. The Concord's faith is not defined as one of Orraveth's four traditions; its religious composition and internal arrangements remain to be established.

Its institutions illustrate the broader tension between spiritual legitimacy and practical governance described in [[political-religious-conflicts|political and religious conflicts]].
