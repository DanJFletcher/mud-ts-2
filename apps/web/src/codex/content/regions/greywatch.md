Greywatch is a fortified outpost of approximately 480 people, including military personnel, about two days southwest of [[bellwold|Bellwold]]. It is under the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]] and military administration. A signal tower came first; the settlement grew around the force assigned to protect nearby trade and enforce royal decrees.

## The outpost

Greywatch investigates banditry, guards routes, and receives orders from the Crown. Its garrison also employs local carriers, cooks, smiths, and suppliers, so the military presence affects civilian trade as well as security. Residents differ on whether the outpost makes travel safer or gives distant authorities too much influence in local affairs.

Captain [[edric-vaul|Edric Vaul]] is a disciplined commander who recognizes that frontier administration is more complicated than a line on a map. Officers under his command do not all agree about how to implement the Crown's orders.

Greywatch began as a fortified signal tower and grew into an outpost with military and civilian work around it. The tower remains its most visible landmark. Residents and posted personnel bring different religious and philosophical traditions; the garrison does not make the settlement's beliefs uniform, and the archive records no single local doctrine.

## Survey orders and competing views

Heskar has ordered surveys of eastern territories for possible future settlement. Some of the lands in question are already used or governed by independent communities. Supporters of surveying argue that accurate records can prevent conflict and improve services; critics fear that measurements will be treated as ownership claims.

The planned [[road-through-old-ground|Road Through Old Ground]] is one point where security, transport, land use, and sovereignty meet. The route and its authority remain disputed. Greywatch's population and travel time are provisional, and this article does not establish a final border or the outcome of the surveying order.
