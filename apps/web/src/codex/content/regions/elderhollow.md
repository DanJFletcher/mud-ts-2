Elderhollow has approximately 130 permanent residents and lies about two days southeast of [[bellwold|Bellwold]]. It is an autonomous woodland community among the oldest continuously inhabited settlements in the region. Homes follow the forms of immense trees; raised walkways and forest paths connect gathering places without imposing a single street plan.

## Assemblies and memory

Elderhollow is governed through assemblies rather than a single ruler. Residents preserve oral histories and maintain relationships with forest sprites, though neither practice is uniform across every household. Decisions about woodland access involve people with different responsibilities, beliefs, and ideas about what should be shared beyond the community.

Elderhollow is among the region's oldest continuously inhabited settlements, but its oral histories do not provide a single agreed founding date. Assemblies, raised walkways, and forest paths are central to its civic life. Residents understand the trees and sprites through varied spiritual and philosophical traditions rather than a single doctrine.

Archivist [[caeren-moss|Caeren Moss]] preserves historical accounts and compares versions rather than treating one remembered telling as complete. Some records describe a period when several forest sprites became silent and portions of the woodland changed. The timing, scope, and cause remain uncertain.

## Bellwold and the older accounts

Those histories may be relevant to [[silent-bell|Bellwold's Silent Bell]], but the connection has not been demonstrated. [[elira-venn|Professor Elira Venn]] was investigating Elderhollow's history shortly before she disappeared. Her interest establishes a line of inquiry, not a solution to either mystery.

Elderhollow trades specialized crafts and historical knowledge with nearby communities, including Bellwold. Residents disagree about how much oral history should be shared with outsiders and what obligations accompany the exchange. Its population, travel time, and status as one of the region's oldest settlements are provisional regional canon.
