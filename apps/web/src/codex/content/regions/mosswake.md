Mosswake is a wetland settlement of approximately 270 people, about a day's travel southeast of [[bellwold|Bellwold]]. Homes and workspaces stand on raised platforms and the scattered areas of firmer ground. The community is independent and has cultural and trading relationships with the [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]].

## Life among the reeds

Fishing, boatbuilding, herbalism, and marsh navigation shape the settlement's work. Narrow channels and shifting water make local knowledge valuable, but no single family owns all knowledge of the wetlands. [[tavin-reed|Tavin Reed]], a young boatbuilder from a family of experienced marsh navigators, is part of a wider community of craftworkers and guides.

Some wetland areas are recognized as sprite territories. The boundaries and meaning of that recognition vary among families and are not represented by a complete map. Residents hold different religious and practical views about these places, including whether access should be governed by custom, consent, or local assembly.

No founding date or single origin story is recorded. Mosswake's raised platforms and paths reflect generations of adapting homes and work to wet ground and shifting channels.

## Fish, marsh, and extraction

Fish populations have declined in parts of the wetlands, though the cause and extent have not been settled. Concentrated [[veyl|Veyl]] deposits have also been found beneath the water. A commercial consortium wants to dredge the area; some residents see possible income and work, while others fear damage to fisheries, channels, and sprite territories.

Mosswake sends fish, reeds, and medicinal plants into regional trade, including toward Bellwold. The products depend on seasonal access and ecological conditions rather than an unlimited supply. Population, travel time, and export relationships are provisional.
