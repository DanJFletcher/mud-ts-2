The Uncrowned Lands are territories beyond centralized national control. Depending on the speaker, they may be called the Wild, the Open Marches, Old Ground, Free Soil, or the Breathing Lands. Each name carries an idea about who belongs there and whether outside authority is legitimate.

These territories are not necessarily uninhabited or available for settlement. They include independent peoples, communities, ruins, and sprite territories. A blank space on a map is a limit in the mapmaker's knowledge, not evidence that no one lives there.

The Codex records no universal border for the Uncrowned Lands. Claims from the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]] or any other state should be read as claims, not as automatic proof of ownership.

The provisional [[bellwold-marches|Bellwold Marches]] lie near these contested edges. Independent communities such as [[thornmere|Thornmere]], [[mosswake|Mosswake]], and [[elderhollow|Elderhollow]] maintain their own forms of governance; their inclusion in a regional directory does not make them part of a state. [[vells-crossing|Vell's Crossing]] disputes Heskar's historical claim. The proposed [[road-through-old-ground|road through Old Ground]] raises questions about the difference between surveying, access, and sovereignty.
