Vell's Crossing is a prosperous settlement of approximately 850 people, about a day and a half east of [[bellwold|Bellwold]]. It grew around a narrow passage through rocky terrain and is an important frontier trading center. Its territory is disputed: the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]] claims a historical connection, while residents do not universally recognize that claim.

## A government of interests

Guild representatives, landholders, and neighborhood delegates make up the town's civic government. Their interests overlap but are not identical. Merchants may favor predictable roads and customs; landholders may prioritize title and access; neighborhood delegates often press for services and safeguards against decisions made without local consent.

[[jarek-vell|Jarek Vell]], a descendant of the settlement's founder, is an influential commercial figure. His family history gives him standing in some conversations, but it does not make him the town's ruler or the sole voice of its residents.

The settlement grew around the narrow passage and its trading yards; the archive records no definitive founding date. Its residents follow multiple religious and philosophical traditions, while civic decisions are made through a mix of commercial, landholding, and neighborhood representation. No one tradition defines the town's political identity.

## Commerce and uncertainty

Imported goods and inexpensive healing potions circulate through the town. Some potions come from legitimate alchemical operations; others have been linked to destructive sprite essence extraction. Commercial agents associated with [[severin-vale|Master Alchemist Severin Vale]] operate here, though most residents do not know the full extent of those connections.

The [[hollow-trade|Hollow Trade]] record describes conflicting evidence and responses. Healers worry about shortages, merchants about disrupted trade, and others about the conditions under which [[veyl|Veyl]] is obtained. The provenance of any particular preparation cannot be inferred from its price or place of sale alone.

Vell's Crossing trades with Bellwold and serves as a distribution point for goods from farther away. Its population, travel time, governance, and political status are provisional regional canon; its claims and affiliations remain contested.
