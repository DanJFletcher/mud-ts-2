Orraveth's political landscape includes monarchies, city-state leagues, federations, cantons, river communities, and territories beyond centralized national control. The [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]] is a hereditary monarchy with rigid borders, strong property law, and expansionist frontier interests. The [[merefall-league|Merefall League]] is a merchant city-state league shaped by guild and commercial influence. The [[veyrun-concord|Veyrun Concord]] balances temple and civic authority; the [[sareth-cantons|Sareth Cantons]] are mountain communities with local assemblies, universities, mining, and skilled craft. The [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]] rely on negotiated councils and reciprocal river rights. These institutions answer different questions about authority, land, and shared resources; none provides a universal model for the continent.

The [[uncrowned-lands|Uncrowned Lands]] are not empty space awaiting a border. Independent peoples, settlements, ruins, and sprite territories exist beyond centralized state claims. Names used for these areas often reveal the speaker's politics.

The archive does not provide a complete map or fixed borders. A town's proximity to a frontier does not establish who owns it, and a state's claim does not make that claim uncontested. [[bellwold|Bellwold]] is a trading settlement near the frontier of Heskar, but its precise legal status is not established. The broader question of how frontier communities live beside different claims is discussed in [[frontier-settlements|Frontier settlements and contested edges]].
