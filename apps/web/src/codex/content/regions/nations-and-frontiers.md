Orraveth's political landscape includes monarchies, city-state leagues, federations, cantons, river communities, and territories beyond centralized national control. The [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]], [[merefall-league|Merefall League]], [[veyrun-concord|Veyrun Concord]], [[sareth-cantons|Sareth Cantons]], and [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]] are not interchangeable systems of authority.

The [[uncrowned-lands|Uncrowned Lands]] are not empty space awaiting a border. Independent peoples, settlements, ruins, and sprite territories exist beyond centralized state claims. Names used for these areas often reveal the speaker's politics.

The archive does not provide a complete map or fixed borders. A town's proximity to a frontier does not establish who owns it, and a state's claim does not make that claim uncontested. [[bellwold|Bellwold]] is a small trading village near the frontier of Heskar.
