The Lower Rill is the provisional name used in the Bellwold Marches record for the river passing near [[bellwold|Bellwold]]. The name provides a local reference, not a surveyed course or a claim that the entire river is governed by one community.

The waterway is important to [[rillford|Rillford]]'s crossing and trade, and the wetlands around [[mosswake|Mosswake]] depend on connected waterways. The exact relationship among these stretches, their seasonal channels, and the broader [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]] is not recorded.

Some Rillford crews associate [[old-silverfin|Old Silverfin]] with the crossing. That local account does not establish the sprite's range or any ownership of the river. Travel, fishing, and passage are governed by changing conditions and local agreements; the archive sets no universal navigation rights or schedule.
