Thornmere is a woodland settlement of approximately 190 people beside a shallow lake, about a day's travel northeast of [[bellwold|Bellwold]]. It is governed by an independent council rather than the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]]. Its residents maintain traditions of coexistence and negotiated agreements with sprites.

## Shared spaces and civic life

Some sprites are said to share buildings, bridges, or communal spaces with residents. These relationships vary; the council does not claim to speak for every sprite, and residents disagree about what a durable agreement requires. Multiple religious and philosophical traditions are present, including people who understand sprite relationships as reciprocal practice and people who prefer a more secular account of shared stewardship.

No founding date is recorded. The community's civic customs are described as having developed through repeated agreements around the lake, its paths, and shared places; the exact history of those agreements remains oral and local.

Elder mediator [[nessa-valeen|Nessa Valeen]] specializes in disputes and agreements between humans and sprites. Her work depends on listening to the parties involved rather than treating a single custom as universal law.

## Medicine and the Veyl prohibition

Thornmere prohibits commercial [[veyl|Veyl]] harvesting. A respected healer has nevertheless purchased conventional essence-based medicine to treat a critically ill child. The account has raised questions about medical necessity, autonomy, enforcement, and the consequences of the community's stated principles. It does not settle whether the purchase was justified or how the council will respond.

Thornmere trades woodland goods, herbs, and specialized knowledge with nearby communities, including Bellwold. Its residents do not all agree that every form of cooperation should be priced or exported. Population, travel time, and local practices described here are provisional.
