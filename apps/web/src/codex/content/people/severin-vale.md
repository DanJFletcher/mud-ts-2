Severin Vale is a wealthy industrialist and master alchemist. He developed an exceptionally efficient but destructive process for extracting sprite essence. Severin calls the process **Refinement**; critics call it [[hollowing|the Hollowing]]. It can strip sprites of memory, agency, and coherent identity.

Severin operates legitimate medical businesses and hospitals while secretly exploiting sprites. His daughter, [[mara-vale|Mara]], has a degenerative illness that requires ongoing treatment. His work began as an attempt to save her, then developed into a profitable industrial system.

Severin argues that ordinary society condemns his methods while depending on the same essence-harvesting industry. The criticism identifies a real contradiction, but does not make his methods harmless or morally acceptable. He is an intellectually serious antagonist whose reasoning and actions remain open to scrutiny.
