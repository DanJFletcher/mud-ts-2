Nessa Valeen is an elder resident of [[thornmere|Thornmere]] known for mediating agreements between humans and sprites. Her work concerns the words people use, what each party believes it has agreed to, and how an arrangement can be revisited when circumstances change.

Nessa does not claim that one custom can govern every sprite or every community. Thornmere's traditions of coexistence include negotiation and shared spaces, but the participants and terms differ. She is wary of promises made on behalf of a being who has not been heard directly.

Thornmere's prohibition on commercial [[veyl|Veyl]] harvesting is a matter of principle and public policy. A healer's purchase of essence-based medicine for a critically ill child has forced the council to consider medical urgency alongside the community's rules. Nessa is concerned that either a rigid response or an unexamined exception could damage trust; the archive does not state what resolution followed.

She exchanges knowledge with neighboring communities, including Bellwold, while remaining rooted in Thornmere's council and relationships. Her own religious tradition and family history have not been recorded.
