Anwen Marr is a 56-year-old merchant who runs [[marrs-general-goods|Marr's General Goods]] in Bellwold. Her shop is part of the everyday trade that supports a village near the frontier.

Anwen owns an unusual [[anwens-balance-scale|balance scale]] that sometimes produces impossible measurements. A weak sprite may be anchored to it, but that explanation is unconfirmed and the scale's exact abilities are unknown.

The scale's mystery does not define Anwen's entire life or business. Her views, family, and full trading history have not been established.

Marr's General Goods participates in Bellwold's local market, which connects the village with nearby farms, workshops, and traveling merchants. The specific suppliers and terms of the shop's regional trade are not recorded; no fixed prices or exchange rules are established.
