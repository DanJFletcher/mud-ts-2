Aven Rusk keeps the records at Bellwold's [[old-grain-house|Old Grain House]]. Chalk marks on the bins and written tallies help distinguish what is stored from what has already left.

Aven records the movement of grain but does not claim that a neat ledger can predict the next harvest.
