Linnet Reed is a Bellwold villager who spends much of the day on foot among the settlement's shared streets and public places. Neighbors see her near the market, South Common, the well, and the Common House, though she keeps no fixed circuit.

Linnet talks easily about daily life and local errands, but has no special knowledge of Bellwold's unresolved mysteries.
