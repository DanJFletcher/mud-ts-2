Maela Thorn is the elected reeve of [[harrowfen|Harrowfen]]. Before taking the office, she worked as an agricultural laborer. Her experience gives her a practical understanding of planting schedules, wages, and the difference between a landholder's account of a field and the work required to keep it productive.

As reeve, Maela negotiates with Heskar landowners, merchants, and neighboring communities on behalf of farmers. She does not control the village's farmland, much of which belongs to noble families, and her authority depends on the confidence of residents as well as the limits of her office.

Maela wants Harrowfen's farmers to have a meaningful voice in decisions about land and harvests. She is concerned by declining yields but does not claim to know their cause. Soil depletion, water management, and altered [[resonance|Resonance]] have all been suggested; the [[vanishing-grain|evidence remains inconclusive]].

Her ties beyond the village are practical: food and flax travel toward [[bellwold|Bellwold]] and through [[rillford|Rillford]], and farm households depend on people who repair tools. The details of her family and private beliefs are not established.
