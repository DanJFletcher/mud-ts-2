Selda Rook is a merchant leader in [[rillford|Rillford]] and a proponent of regulated river commerce. She argues that agreed procedures for loading, passage, and dispute resolution protect small traders as well as large firms.

Not everyone trusts the regulations she supports. Independent boatmen fear that a ferry guild will turn rules into an exclusive right, while customs officials want the crossing to remain legible to the [[crown-of-heskar|Crown of Heskar]]. Selda's position is not the unanimous view of Rillford's merchants, much less the whole river corridor.

Reports that [[old-silverfin|Old Silverfin]] overturns vessels carrying certain cargoes complicate her plans. Selda wants cargoes and routes documented before anyone draws conclusions, but she has not identified a common cause or motive. She treats the incidents as a matter requiring careful inquiry rather than proof of a particular accusation.

Her business connects Rillford with [[bellwold|Bellwold]] and other markets. No record establishes her family history, religious commitments, or private relationships beyond her civic and commercial role.
