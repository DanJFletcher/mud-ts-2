Enna Pell keeps notices in Bellwold's [[bellwold-common-house|Common House]] and copies agreed decisions into the village record book. She distinguishes what was recorded from what each person hoped would happen.

The current record does not establish that Enna holds formal authority over Bellwold. Her role is to preserve public notes, not to speak for every resident.
