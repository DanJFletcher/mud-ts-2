Rell Soven builds and repairs cart wheels, axles, and wooden fittings in a yard off [[wattle-row|Wattle Row]]. Their work is practical and often unnoticed until a cart begins to wobble on a long road.

Rell's knowledge concerns the construction and wear of wheels. They do not claim to know what moves the [[abandoned-watermill|abandoned watermill]] when its channel is low.
