Caeren Moss is an archivist in [[elderhollow|Elderhollow]], responsible for preserving the community's historical accounts. Much of that history has been carried orally. Caeren records who told a story, what version they learned, and where memories differ rather than smoothing every disagreement into one chronology.

Some accounts describe a period when several forest sprites became silent and parts of the woodland changed. The tellings vary in their timing and detail. Caeren treats them as important testimony, not as a complete explanation of a later event.

[[elira-venn|Professor Elira Venn]] was investigating Elderhollow's history shortly before she disappeared near Bellwold. Caeren's records may bear on that inquiry, but neither the archive nor the scholar's absence establishes a connection to [[silent-bell|Bellwold's Silent Bell]]. Caeren is careful not to make the records say more than their tellers knew.

As an archivist, Caeren is also concerned with what can be shared outside Elderhollow. Some histories are communal knowledge; others belong to particular families or relationships. The terms of any exchange with Bellwold remain a matter for the people involved.
