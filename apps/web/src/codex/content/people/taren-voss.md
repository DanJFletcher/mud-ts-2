Taren Voss serves as a watch guard in Bellwold. The route links the market square and the north road with South Common, Well Court, and the Common House before returning to the square.

Taren keeps his attention on crossings and the ordinary movement of people. The current record does not establish a larger watch hierarchy or any special authority beyond his patrol.
