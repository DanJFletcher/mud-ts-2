Oren Kest is a carpenter in [[ashcombe|Ashcombe]], where woodcraft supports construction, carts, and the tools used across the region. He will not work with timber taken from certain areas. The boundary of his refusal is known to his suppliers but has not been recorded in a complete public map.

Oren's decision reflects both his craft and his view that a harvest should be judged by where and how the tree was taken, not only by its quality. Some Ashcombe workers share his concern; others believe the new Heskar concessions create needed employment and can be managed responsibly.

After workers felled an old oak whose cut surface appeared to bleed clear sap, some reported hearing voices in the lumber. Oren has heard the accounts but does not claim to know whether the oak housed a sprite or whether the voices have another cause. His caution toward certain stands predates no established explanation in the archive.

The proposed [[road-through-old-ground|road through Old Ground]] worries him because a survey line can become a timber boundary without the agreement of everyone who uses the forest. Oren trades his work through regional markets, including Bellwold; he is not a representative of all woodland communities.
