Tavin Reed is a young boatbuilder in [[mosswake|Mosswake]], raised in a family whose members have long navigated the wetlands. He knows the work of fitting a hull to shallow channels and the patience required to repair a boat after a season of hard use.

Tavin's family has connections to the wider river communities of the [[rillward-freeholds|Rillward Freeholds]], but neither that relationship nor his age makes him a spokesperson for Mosswake. He is still building his own standing among other craftworkers and marsh travelers.

The fish decline and proposed dredging of Veyl deposits have placed practical pressure on his work. Dredging might bring contracts and new trade, but could alter channels on which navigation and fishing depend. Tavin is concerned about decisions made without marsh knowledge; he has not declared that every form of extraction must be refused.

He trades boats and repairs within the wetlands and toward Bellwold. His specific religious commitments, family members, and position on the consortium's proposal remain unrecorded.
