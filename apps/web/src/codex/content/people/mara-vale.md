Mara Vale is the daughter of [[severin-vale|Severin Vale]]. That family connection is the only firmly established fact in the current record.

Accounts of her life, choices, and views should not be inferred from her father's research. The Codex leaves her biography open for future material rather than assigning her an unrecorded role in the disputes over [[essence-harvesting|sprite essence]].
