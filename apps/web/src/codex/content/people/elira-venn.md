Professor Elira Venn is a 43-year-old natural philosopher associated with the [[glass-collegium|Glass Collegium]]. She specializes in Resonance measurement and incorporeal sprites. Colleagues describe her method as skeptical and careful; she is interested in instruments that can measure sprite activity.

Her research suggests that repeated essence extraction may damage a sprite's identity. That is an evidence-based concern, not a complete explanation of every case. While investigating the mysterious bell near [[bellwold|Bellwold]], she disappeared. A [[forgotten-journal|journal attributed to her]] contains unusual measurements, missing historical records, and a mysterious voice.

Elira is missing, not confirmed dead. Neither the bell nor her disappearance has a verified explanation. Any account that assigns a definitive cause, culprit, or fate goes beyond the available record.
