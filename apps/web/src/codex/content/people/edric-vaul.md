Captain Edric Vaul commands the Heskar garrison at [[greywatch|Greywatch]]. He is disciplined and expects orders to be carried out, but he recognizes that a decree written far from the frontier may intersect with local agreements, land use, and histories absent from official files.

Edric is responsible for security and the implementation of royal instructions. Those duties do not make him the author of every policy, nor do they resolve whether a policy is legitimate in territories whose residents contest Heskar's claim. Officers at Greywatch disagree about how strictly to interpret the Crown's orders.

The survey of eastern territory and the proposed [[road-through-old-ground|road project]] concern him because both could improve military mobility while creating conflict with communities already living on or using the land. Edric favors careful records but knows that a survey can be read as a claim. His approach and the eventual outcome are not established.

The surviving record gives little about his life before Greywatch or his personal beliefs. He works with Bellwold and other communities through security and supply arrangements, not as a neutral voice for every resident.
