Seli Tor weaves and mends cloth at [[tallow-end-cottage|Tallow End Cottage]], taking on neighbors' work when the loom is free. Much of the work keeps everyday things useful: patched sacks, repaired sleeves, and cloth made to last through travel.

Seli's wider family, training, and ties beyond [[wattle-row|Wattle Row]] are not yet established.
