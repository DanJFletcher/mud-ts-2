An alchemical preparation is more than its finished liquid. Practitioners record ingredient quality, proportions, equipment, storage, and the conditions under which a batch was made. An identical label can conceal meaningful differences in method.

For medicines containing [[veyl|Veyl]], provenance includes the way the essence was obtained. A responsible record distinguishes [[essence-harvesting|ambient collection, voluntary giving, cultivation, and destructive extraction]] rather than hiding all four behind a supplier's seal.

Small workshops and large institutions do not always agree on how much documentation is practical. The Glass Collegium's published methods influence standards, but local knowledge and independent verification remain important. This entry describes a developing convention, not a continent-wide licensing code.
