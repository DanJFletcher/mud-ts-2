Healing potions are alchemical preparations that support accelerated healing. Many use [[veyl|Veyl]], though a bottle's label does not always make its source or preparation clear.

Potions are medicine, not a guarantee against injury, infection, or death. Strength, freshness, dose, and a patient's condition all matter. Responsible healers keep records of what was administered and monitor for unexpected effects rather than treating a successful case as universal proof.

The availability of a potion can depend on trade, local laws, and the cost of ethically sourced ingredients. The [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]] seeks alternatives that would reduce dependence on sprite essence, but its treatments remain experimental and expensive.
