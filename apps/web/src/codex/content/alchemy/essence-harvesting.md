Records distinguish four broad ways to obtain Veyl: ambient collection, voluntary giving, cultivated production, and destructive extraction. The categories matter because they describe different relationships, risks, and claims of consent.

Ambient collection may have no identifiable source; that uncertainty is not proof that it is harmless. Voluntary giving depends on whether a sprite can understand and refuse the request. Cultivation aims to produce essence without injury, but methods and outcomes differ. Destructive extraction can harm or kill a sprite and is condemned by many practitioners, though enforcement and public opinion vary by jurisdiction.

The [[essence-provenance|provenance of a preparation]] is therefore central to ethical review. The disputed condition called [[hollowing|Hollowing]] is one reason many researchers argue for alternatives such as the [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]].
