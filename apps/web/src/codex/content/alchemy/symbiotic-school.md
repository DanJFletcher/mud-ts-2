The Symbiotic School studies cooperative relationships with sprites that may choose to contribute to healing work. Its members argue that cooperation must be sustained by clear consent, the ability to refuse, and benefit that is not defined only by human need.

The School's approach is distinct from treating a sprite as a naturally renewable supply. A relationship can change, and prior agreement does not grant permanent access. Practical standards are still being developed, particularly when a sprite's communication differs from human speech.

The work connects questions of [[sprite-consent-and-personhood|personhood]] with the [[essence-harvesting|ethics of obtaining Veyl]]. It remains experimental; no single symbiotic arrangement is assumed to work for every sprite.
