Hollowing is a term used for suspected lasting harm to a sprite after destructive essence extraction. Reports describe diminished response, altered memory, or a loss of familiar behavior, but they are difficult to compare and may reflect different causes.

The name is not a settled diagnosis. Some researchers see a pattern consistent with injury; others note the lack of reliable baselines and the difficulty of observing a sprite over time. Economic interests can influence which cases are recorded, preserved, or dismissed.

Whatever the final explanation, uncertainty is not proof of safety. The debate informs [[essence-harvesting|harvesting ethics]], [[sprite-consent-and-personhood|consent]], and research into alternatives such as the [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]]. The existence or mechanism of Hollowing remains unresolved.
