The Botanical School develops plant-based remedies as part of the [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]]. Its researchers document cultivation conditions, preparation, dose, and observed effects, since a remedy can vary with species, soil, season, and storage.

The School works with growers and local healers, whose knowledge may precede institutional records by generations. Researchers disagree about how to balance controlled trials with the need to respect that experience.

Botanical treatments are not automatically safer or more effective than preparations containing [[veyl|Veyl]]. The School's goal is to expand the available choices and reduce dependence on sprite essence, not to claim that one method suits every patient.
