Resonance Engineers are the instrument-makers and experimental practitioners associated with the [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]]. They seek treatments that apply [[resonance|Resonance]] without consuming sprite essence.

Their work depends on reliable anchors, repeatable patterns, and careful attention to energy and scale. A promising device must be tested for failure as well as success, especially when it will be used on an injured person.

The field overlaps with the research of the [[glass-collegium|Glass Collegium]], though engineers are often judged by practical outcomes rather than a complete theory. Their methods remain experimental and do not make essence-free medicine broadly accessible yet.
