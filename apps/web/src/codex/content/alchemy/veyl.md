Veyl is the name used for sprite essence in alchemical practice. It can be used in preparations that support accelerated healing, making it valuable to healers, merchants, and researchers.

The term does not identify one standardized substance or one universally accepted theory. Its quality and effects are discussed in relation to source, handling, and preparation. Records often fail to disclose whether the essence was [[essence-harvesting|ambient, voluntarily given, cultivated, or destructively taken]].

The costs of destructive extraction are a source of concern. Some scholars use [[hollowing|Hollowing]] for suspected harm to a sprite, while others dispute whether existing observations establish a consistent syndrome. Veyl remains materially useful and ethically contested; its usefulness does not settle the status of the being it came from.

In the provisional [[bellwold-marches|Bellwold Marches]], Veyl is part of several distinct disputes. [[thornmere|Thornmere]] prohibits commercial harvesting but faces a medical exception; [[mosswake|Mosswake]] is weighing a proposal to dredge a wetland deposit; and Harrowfen has received a grain offer tied to extraction rights. These accounts do not establish that a single operation connects the cases or that Veyl explains the harvest decline.
