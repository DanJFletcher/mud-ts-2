Provenance is the recorded path of Veyl from source to preparation. A useful record identifies who collected or supplied it, what method was used, how it was stored, and what uncertainties remain. It can also identify whether a sprite participated voluntarily, where that can be known.

The chain is difficult to verify when essence passes through several brokers or crosses a jurisdiction with different [[sprite-law-and-culture|rules for sprite status]]. Sellers may use broad labels that obscure a difference between cultivation and destructive extraction.

Provenance cannot by itself prove consent or guarantee safety, but missing records are a reason for caution. The practice is an emerging standard, not a single law shared by all of Orraveth.
