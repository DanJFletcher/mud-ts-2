Most healing does not begin with a rare potion. Rest, clean water, careful wound care, plant remedies, skilled attendants, and time remain important in communities across Orraveth. Their availability is shaped by wealth, distance, training, and local ecology.

The [[unbinding-initiative|Unbinding Initiative]] studies treatments that avoid sprite essence. Its approaches include botanical remedies, Resonance engineering, and cooperative work with sprites. These methods are experimental, expensive, and not yet a universal replacement for established preparations.

The distinction between “traditional” and “scientific” care is often overstated. Local practitioners preserve useful observations, while institutional researchers can test specific claims under controlled conditions. The best records note uncertainty and avoid promising a cure where evidence is limited.
