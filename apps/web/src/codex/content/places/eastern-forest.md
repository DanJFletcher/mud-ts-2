The eastern forest lies beyond Bellwold. Local records associate it with the origin of the [[silent-bell|old bell]], which is said to have come from the forest before being installed in the village tower.

The extent of the woodland, its paths, and the communities that use it have not been established in the current archive. It should not be treated as empty simply because Bellwold's written records are limited.

Its frontier setting places it near questions of land, authority, and local knowledge that also shape the [[uncrowned-lands|Uncrowned Lands]].
