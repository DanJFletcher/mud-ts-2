Tallow End Cottage stands at the southern end of [[wattle-row|Wattle Row]]. A loom sits near the window, with folded cloth and mending kept clear of the hearth.

[[seli-tor|Seli Tor]] weaves and repairs cloth there, taking on work from neighbors when time allows. The cottage's name and details are provisional.
