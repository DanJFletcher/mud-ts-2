Bellwold's old bell tower houses the bell known as the [[silent-bell|Silent Bell]]. Historical accounts say it rang before storms and, in a period when seventeen villagers disappeared, rang continuously.

The bell is said to have come from the [[eastern-forest|eastern forest]]. Its ringing stopped after [[elira-venn|Professor Elira Venn]] began measuring its [[resonance|Resonance]]. The sequence is documented; whether one event caused the other is not known.

The tower is a subject of local memory and scholarly attention, not a solved monument. Its original purpose and the bell's true nature remain unresolved.
