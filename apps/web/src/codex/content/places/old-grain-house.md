The Old Grain House is raised on a stone sill with high ventilation gaps along its timber walls. Chalk marks divide its bins by season rather than by owner.

[[aven-rusk|Aven Rusk]] keeps its records and checks that stored grain remains dry and accounted for. The storehouse stands east of [[well-court|Well Court]].
