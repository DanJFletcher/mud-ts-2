The Kindly Roof is Bellwold's inn, kept by [[tomas|Tomas]]. It offers travelers a place to sleep and a place where news, trade, and local memory meet.

Local accounts speak of the [[kindly-roof-hearth|Hearth of the Kindly Roof]], a presence associated with the inn's hearth. Elira Venn's [[forgotten-journal|journal was discovered here]], though the circumstances of its appearance remain part of the mystery.

The inn is a working business in a small trading village, not a grand institution. Its hospitality matters because Bellwold sits near a frontier where visitors and residents depend on one another's knowledge.

Travelers reaching Bellwold bring news from the settlements of the provisional [[bellwold-marches|Bellwold Marches]]. The inn is one place where accounts from [[harrowfen|Harrowfen]], [[rillford|Rillford]], and more distant communities may be exchanged; no regular route or schedule is established.
