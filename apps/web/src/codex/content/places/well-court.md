Well Court is a small public space organized around a stone-rimmed well and a broad trough. Residents come for water and pause to speak with neighbors.

The court connects [[bellwold-south-common|South Common]] to [[wattle-row|Wattle Row]], the [[old-grain-house|Old Grain House]], and the [[bellwold-common-house|Common House]]. Its present name and arrangement are provisional.
