Bellwold Common House is a plain timber meeting room furnished with a long table, benches, and a wall for public notices. Residents use it to discuss shared work and preserve the wording of agreements.

[[enna-pell|Enna Pell]] keeps the notices and copies agreed decisions into the village record book. The house stands south of [[well-court|Well Court]] and west of [[tallow-end-cottage|Tallow End Cottage]].
