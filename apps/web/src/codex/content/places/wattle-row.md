Wattle Row is a narrow residential lane of modest homes and small work yards. Its walls are patched with local materials as needed, giving the street a practical rather than uniform appearance.

The lane reaches [[well-court|Well Court]] to the east and [[green-mortar|the Green Mortar]] to the north. [[sovens-wheelwright-yard|Soven's Wheelwright Yard]] and [[tallow-end-cottage|Tallow End Cottage]] open onto it.
