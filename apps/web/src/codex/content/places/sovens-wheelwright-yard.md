Soven's Wheelwright Yard is a fenced work space on [[wattle-row|Wattle Row]]. A turning frame, spare spokes, and rims in different stages of repair reflect the everyday need to keep carts moving.

[[rell-soven|Rell Soven]] builds and repairs wheels, axles, and wooden fittings. The yard's particular history has not been established.
