The abandoned watermill stands outside Bellwold. Though deserted, it continues to operate, making it a central subject of the [[mill-that-never-sleeps|Mill That Never Sleeps]] mystery.

The concealed account links the mill to an extraction apparatus using its resident sprite. An agent connected to [[severin-vale|Severin Vale]] is using the apparatus, and the resulting Veyl is entering the regional [[healing-potions|healing-potion supply chain]]. This information is not established as common knowledge among Bellwold's residents.

The resident sprite's identity and circumstances, the agent's identity, and the apparatus's full operation remain unspecified. The mill's continued motion should not be mistaken for proof that the sprite consents.
