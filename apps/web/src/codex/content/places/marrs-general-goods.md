Marr's General Goods is a Bellwold shop operated by [[anwen-marr|Anwen Marr]]. It serves the ordinary needs of a trading village where farming, craft, travel, and seasonal supplies overlap.

Anwen owns an unusual [[anwens-balance-scale|balance scale]] that sometimes produces impossible measurements. A weak sprite may be anchored to it, but the possibility has not been confirmed and its abilities are unknown.

The shop is part of Bellwold's local economy, not a source of fixed continent-wide prices. The Codex does not assign denominations or exchange rates to Orraveth's currencies.
