Blackwater Forge is the Bellwold smithy, where [[darric-holt|Darric Holt]] works as a blacksmith. Its work supports farming, repair, construction, and the movement of goods through the frontier village.

The forge is one of several small enterprises that make Bellwold resilient. Its production depends on fuel, ore, tools, and skilled labor, linking it to supply networks that extend well beyond the village.

The details of the forge's history and any unusual craft methods are not established in the present record.
